Every recipe you love, minus the life story.

Sizzle is a cookbook you run yourself. Share a recipe page from your phone and it keeps the ingredients, the steps and the best photo. The ads, pop-ups and life story stay behind.

Free and open source under the AGPL. Runs in one small Docker container.

How it works

  1. Share

    Save a recipe from your phone’s share sheet, or paste several links at once. Sharing guides cover iPhone, Android and computers.

  2. Sizzle cleans it up

    Sizzle reads a page’s recipe data without AI. You can also write recipes yourself, or enable Claude to handle pasted text and pages without recipe data.

  3. Cook from your collection

    Browse a photo grid or a compact list. Search by ingredient, or filter by course, cuisine, diet and time. “I made this” keeps track of when and how often you cook each recipe.

YouTube works too. Sizzle reads recipes from video descriptions or the recipe pages they link to. Recipes that are only spoken in the video aren’t supported yet.

Try your first cookbook

With Docker running, start a local trial. No account or AI key is needed. Only this computer can connect, and the trial keeps its recipes in a separate storage volume.

Write a recipe or load the optional sample cookbook, try cooking mode, then download your recipes from Settings. Phone sharing can wait until you install on your server.

Sample recipes and trial guide 

docker run -d --name sizzle-try \
  -p 127.0.0.1:3001:80 \
  --read-only --security-opt no-new-privileges:true \
  --tmpfs /rails/tmp:uid=1000,gid=1000,mode=0755 \
  --tmpfs /tmp:mode=1777 \
  -v sizzle-try-storage:/rails/storage \
  ghcr.io/leemac/sizzle:latest

Allow a moment for startup, then open http://localhost:3001.

Stop with docker stop sizzle-try; resume with docker start sizzle-try. Both keep your recipes. The removal guide explains how to delete the trial and its data.

Yours, on your own server

Run Sizzle in one Docker container on a home server, NAS or Raspberry Pi. Your recipes and photos stay on your server, with no tracking or Sizzle subscription.

Sizzle has no login yet. Keep it on your home network, or use Tailscale to reach it away from home. Optional Claude extraction uses your own API key and is billed by Anthropic.

Tech specs

  • One Docker container
  • SQLite · no external database
  • amd64 and arm64
  • No AI required

1. Download the files

With Docker and Compose installed:

mkdir sizzle && cd sizzle
url=https://github.com/leemac/sizzle/releases/latest/download
curl -fsSLO $url/docker-compose.yml
curl -fsSL $url/env.example -o .env

2. Set your share token

openssl rand -hex 32

Open .env in a text editor, paste the generated value after SIZZLE_API_TOKEN=, and save. This protects sharing requests, not the web UI. Leave the Anthropic key empty to try without AI.

3. Start your cookbook

docker compose up -d --wait

When the health check passes, open http://<your-server>:3000. On the server itself, use http://localhost:3000.
